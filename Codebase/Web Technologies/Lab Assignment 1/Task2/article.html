Your Password Is Weaker Than You Think

By Sara Malik · 3 October 2026

Most of us were taught to swap letters for symbols and call it secure. Attackers know every one of those tricks, and their tools try them first.

What really protects an account is length. A passphrase made of four or five unrelated words is easy to remember and far harder to guess than a short, complicated string.

Pair it with a password manager and two-factor authentication, and never reuse it across sites. One leaked password should never open every door.
