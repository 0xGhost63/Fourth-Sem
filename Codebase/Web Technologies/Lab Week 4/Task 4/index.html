Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, shipping, and support.

What is your warranty policy?

All TechGear products come with a 2-year full manufacturer warranty covering hardware defects, power issues, and component replacements.

How fast is express shipping?

Express shipping orders placed before 2 PM are processed on the same day and delivered within 24 to 48 hours nationwide.

Can I return an item if I change my mind?

Yes, we offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy. Simply return the product in its original packaging with proof of purchase for a full refund.

Do you provide international shipping?

We ship worldwide to over 50 countries. Shipping rates and estimated delivery dates are calculated at checkout based on your destination.
